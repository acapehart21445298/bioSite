Music

Music is an important part of David's life. After more than 30 years away from the violin, he decided to return to playing. His involvement with CFCArts gives him an opportunity to enjoy music while connecting with others in the community.

Cycling

Cycling is another way David enjoys staying active. It offers an opportunity to spend time outdoors, challenge himself, and enjoy the experience of being on the move.

Running and Giving Back

David also enjoys running and participating in races. Beyond the physical challenge, these events can provide opportunities to support charitable causes and become involved in the community.
